Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries

To apply for a bus pass, first register an account or login if you already have one. Then navigate to your Dashboard and click on "Apply for Pass". Fill in your personal details, select your route and pass type, upload a photo and ID proof, and submit your application. An admin will review and approve your application.
Once your pass has expired, go to your Dashboard and find the expired pass. Click the "Renew Pass" button. You can select a new duration (Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, Student, or Senior Citizen). A new application will be submitted with your existing details. After admin approval, proceed to payment to activate your renewed pass.
After your pass application is approved by an admin, a "Proceed to Payment" button will appear on your dashboard. Click it to open the payment form. Enter your card details. Upon successful payment, your pass will be activated and a PDF will be generated for download.
If your application is rejected, you will see the "Rejected" status on your dashboard. Common reasons include incomplete details or invalid documents. You can submit a new application with corrected information. If you believe the rejection was in error, please contact our support team via the Support Form below.
We offer the following types of passes:
• Weekly Pass (₹150): Valid for 7 days. Ideal for short-term users.
• Monthly Pass (₹500): Valid for 30 days from activation.
• 3-Month Pass (₹1,350): Valid for 90 days with a 10% discount (saves ₹150).
• 6-Month Pass (₹2,700): Valid for 180 days with a 10% discount (saves ₹300).
• Student Pass (₹400): Valid for 30 days. Requires student ID verification.
• Senior Citizen Pass (₹350): Valid for 30 days. For passengers aged 60+.
All passes include unlimited rides on your selected route and come as a digital PDF.
After successful payment, your digital bus pass is automatically generated as a PDF. Go to your Dashboard, find your active pass, and click the "Download Pass" button. The PDF includes your name, route, validity dates, photo (if uploaded), and pass ID.
Once you submit your application, it is reviewed by our admin team. Typically, applications are processed within 24 to 48 hours during working days. You will see the updated status (Approved or Rejected) on your Dashboard. After approval, you can proceed to payment and activate your pass immediately.
The following documents may be required during application:
• Photo: A recent passport-size photograph (JPG or PNG format).
• ID Proof: Any valid government-issued ID such as Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License.
• Student ID: Required if applying for a Student Pass — upload your college/school ID card.
• Age Proof: Required for Senior Citizen Pass — any document showing date of birth.
Photo and ID uploads are optional during application but recommended for faster approval.
Yes! You can apply for multiple bus passes for different routes or durations. Each application is processed independently. For example, you can have a Monthly Pass for your daily commute (e.g., Dadar → Andheri) and a Weekly Pass for occasional travel on a different route. All your passes are managed from your Dashboard, where you can track their status, make payments, and download pass PDFs.
